How this works

A TV that only gets music channels. Turn it on, flip around, leave it on. It pays attention.

How it learns

Every flip is a vote. Bail on a video in the first few seconds and the set notes it. Stick around, or hit LOVE, and channel 2 (MTV YOU) starts programming for you. Every other channel quietly reorders its upcoming picks around your taste too, without losing its genre.

It all lives in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with MTV, VH1, or Paramount. Videos play from YouTube through the official embed; some may be unavailable in your region and the set skips them. Reset what it has learned
